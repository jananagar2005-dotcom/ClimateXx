Oil Spills & Marine Pollution

A tanker ran aground off the coast of Mauritius in 2020 and leaked thousands of tonnes of fuel into the Indian Ocean. The vessel later split in two, releasing more oil and threatening coral reefs, marine wildlife, and coastal ecosystems

Deforestation & Forest Loss

Forests are being cut down at an alarming rate to make space for agriculture, roads, and urban development. The loss of forests reduces biodiversity, destroys natural habitats, and releases stored carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, making climate change worse

Gold Mining & Mercury Pollution

In La Rinconada, one of the world's highest gold-mining settlements, mercury is used to help extract gold from ore. This process can release toxic mercury into the surrounding environment, contaminating water and soil and posing serious risks to wildlife and local ecosystems

Factory Pollution & Toxic Waste

In 1984, a pesticide factory in Bhopal, India, released a large amount of toxic gas into the surrounding city. The disaster caused widespread environmental contamination and exposed nearby communities to dangerous pollution, becoming one of the world's worst industrial accidents
